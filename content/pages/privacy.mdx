<QuickAnswer>
Walang account dito, walang login, at wala kaming hinihinging pangalan mo. Dalawang klase lang ng
cookie ang meron: iyong kailangan para gumana ang site, at ang Google Analytics — optional iyon at
naka-off hangga't hindi ka pumayag. Walang advertising cookie, walang ad network, at walang
binebenta o ipinapasa sa iba.
</QuickAnswer>

## The short version

We do not know who you are. There is no sign-up on this site, no comment box, no newsletter, and
no form that asks for your name. Wala kaming hawak na dokumento mo — hindi kami notaryo at hindi
kami platform.

The only thing we would like to know is which pages people read, so we can tell which explanations
are working and which ones are wasting your time. That is counted with Google Analytics, and it
only runs if you say yes.

## What is set before you choose anything

Two things, and both stay on your own device:

- **Your light or dark setting.** Stored in your browser so the page does not flash white at night.
- **Your cookie choice itself.** Stored so we do not ask you again on every page. Ironically, saying
  no requires us to remember that you said no.

Neither is sent to us or to anyone else. Walang analytics dito, walang tracking.

## What Google Analytics collects, if you allow it

If — and only if — you turn analytics on, Google Analytics records the ordinary things a page
counter records:

| What | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Which page you opened | `/guides/ofw` |
| Where you came from | a Google search, a Facebook post, or typed directly |
| Roughly where you are | country and region, not your address |
| What device and browser | "Android, Chrome" |
| Approximate visit length | how long the page stayed open |

It sets cookies named `_ga` and `_ga_*` on your browser, which last about two years and exist to
avoid counting the same browser twice.

**What it does not collect from us:** your name, your email, your phone number, your documents, or
anything you typed into a tool on this site. The calculators and checklists on `/tools` run entirely
in your browser — the numbers you enter never leave your device and never reach a server, ours or
Google's.

Google is the processor here, not us, and it operates outside the Philippines. Basahin mo ang
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) kung gusto mong makita ang panig
nila.

## No advertising, ever

We set no advertising cookies. We run no ads, we have no ad network, we do no remarketing, and we
have nothing to sell you. Ad storage, ad personalisation and ad user data are denied by default in
our Google configuration and there is no switch anywhere on this site that turns them on, because
there is nothing to turn on.

Kung may nakita kang ad na parang galing dito, hindi iyon sa amin. Read [Safety and scams](/safety).

## How consent actually works here

Before Google Analytics is even allowed to load its own settings, this site tells Google that
analytics storage is **denied**. That instruction runs first, on every page load, before the
analytics script starts. It is not a banner that merely hides a tracker that is already running —
denial is the starting state, and it only changes when you change it.

When you allow analytics, we send a single update that flips analytics storage to granted. When you
withdraw, it flips back and the `_ga` cookies are cleared.

That control opens the same panel as the banner, on any page, at any time. Turning analytics off
takes effect immediately and clears the analytics cookies.

You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or use your browser's private window. The site
works fully either way — nothing here is gated behind analytics.

## Server logs

Like every website, our host records the requests it serves: IP address, timestamp, the page
requested, and the browser's user-agent string. This is how a server works and it cannot be switched
off without switching off the site. Ginagamit lang ito para sa seguridad at para malaman kung may
sira, hindi para sundan ka.

## Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) gives you rights over personal information
about you — among them the right to be informed, to access, to object, and to erasure. This page is
our attempt at the "informed" part in plain language.

In practice we hold almost nothing to give you: no account, no profile, no mailing list. If you
believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will tell you what we have, or confirm that
we have nothing. Kung hindi ka kuntento sa sagot namin, pwede kang dumulog sa National Privacy
Commission.
